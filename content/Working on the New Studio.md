The decluttering continues, especially as it's hot and smokey out. The new space in the basement that I'm clearing to move all my sewing into is big enough to contain So Many Activities. Check out the layout gameplan.

![[910881C1-893D-4F33-A46C-C232BB4A905D.heic]]

The room is long with a working fireplace. Working from right to left in the sketch:

1. **Tailoring Table**: This is a large table that I do all of my tailoring and hand sewing at. It's a perfect height to stand at and work, but has some high stools for sitting. On one wall I plan to put shelving for cloth and notions and the other wall will be where I hang patterns. All of my paper patterns get large holes punched in them so I can hang them on bars mounted on the wall.
2. **Weaving**: In the middle of the room will be three of those IKEA workstation units that have shelves with long tables attached to them. The tables/shelves will be devoted to weaving, with room for my table loom and a tapestry loom I have planned. Shelves for yarn and such behind.
3. **Sewing Machines**: The third shelves/table unit will be for modern sewing. There's a regular sewing machine and a serger planned there, with room for modern fabrics and notions.
4. **Cozy Chairs and Shallow Shelves**: I hope I have room for two comfy chairs in front of the fireplace for knitting and sitting to do hand work there. So nice if so. The space along the wall there is not deep but can handle shallow shelves for knitting yarn and other stuff.
5. **Art**: And finally in the top left corner will be my art table for watercolor, illumination, and charcoal. There will probably be some storage there but I want to move everything else in and then see what I can do.

The old sewing room is all the way to the left through a door near the shallow shelves; that will be the new gym. Currently the gym equipment is in the area where the tailoring is and it's taken over by clutter. Devoting that room to exercise will help me just go down and Do It.

I'm also going to fire up the new studio for streaming and get on my Tinker Tailor TV aspirations. I'll be streaming on Twitch and YouTube and will let everyone know when that starts up!
